**How to Connect to Oracle DB**

***Steps to Connect:***

1. Open SQL*Plus.*
    
2. Enter the username and password.
    

***Method 1: Enter separately***

```
Username : SYSTEM
Password : NARESH
```

***Method 2: Enter together***

```
SYSTEM/NARESH
```

==🔴Note: `SYSTEM` is the username, and `NARESH` is the password.==
