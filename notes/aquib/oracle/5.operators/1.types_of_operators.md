==🟢**Operators in Oracle**==

- Arithmetic Operators: `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`
- Relational Operators: `>`, `>=`, `<`, `<=`, `=`, `<>`, `!=`
- Logical Operators: `AND`, `OR`, `NOT`
- Special Operators: `BETWEEN`, `IN`, `LIKE`, `IS NULL`, `ANY`, `ALL`, `EXISTS`
- Set Operators: `UNION`, `UNION ALL`, `INTERSECT`, `MINUS`
- Concatenation Operator: `||`
