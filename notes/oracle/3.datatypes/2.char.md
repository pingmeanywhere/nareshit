`CHAR(size)`

- *Stores fixed-length character data up to 2000 bytes.*
- *Recommended for fixed-length fields.*
- *Extra space is wasted when the data is shorter than the specified size.*

***Example:***

```sql
NAME CHAR(10)
```

- `SACHIN` → 6 characters + 4 unused spaces
- `RAVI` → 4 characters + 6 unused spaces

***Examples of fixed-length fields:***

```sql
GENDER       CHAR(1)  -- M, F
STATE_CODE   CHAR(2)  -- AP, TG
COUNTRY_CODE CHAR(3)  -- IND, USA
```

==🔴Note: `CHAR` is not recommended for variable-length fields.==
